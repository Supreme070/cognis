Logo & Identity Guidelines

Version 1.0 · August 2026 · Applies to all Cognis Group touchpoints: web, product, print, video, and social.

This document defines the Cognis Group mark, its lockups, colors, typography, spacing, motion behavior, and rules of use. Follow it exactly; consistency is what makes a mark a brand. When a situation isn't covered here, choose the simplest option that preserves legibility and contrast.

1 · The mark: the C ring

The mark is a C for Cognis drawn as an open loop. The ring is deliberately not closed: cognition is iterative — a cycle of learning, testing, and refining that never claims to be finished. The lime keystone sits in the opening: it is the piece Cognis adds. The client's problem is the open ring; Cognis is the part that completes it. The rigid square meeting the fluid circle also carries the company motto Quod Tango Muto — "what I touch, I transform."

1.1 Construction

  • Drawn on a 48 × 48 unit grid; ring center at (24, 24), outer radius 22, inner radius 12 (ring weight ≈ 10 units).
  • The notch flares outward: the cut spans 32° each side of horizontal at the outer edge and 40° at the inner edge, so the keystone sits in a fitted slot rather than a straight slice.
  • The keystone is a 10 × 10 unit square, optically centered in the notch at the ring's horizontal axis. It is always a sharp square — never rounded.
  • Never redraw, re-proportion, or reconstruct the mark. Use only the supplied SVG/PNG files.

There are three levels of the identity. Use the shortest form the context allows — formality goes up, length goes up.

2.1 Orientation variants

  • Horizontal (mark left of wordmark) is the default in all contexts.
  • Stacked (mark above wordmark, centered) only where width is constrained: square social tiles, narrow banners, spine of a document.
  • Gap between mark and wordmark: 0.28 × mark height. Mark height ≈ 1.25 × wordmark cap height.

3.1 Color rules by background

  • Light backgrounds (white, paper): ink ring + lime keystone. Full color is the default.
  • Dark backgrounds (ink, near-black): white ring + lime keystone.
  • Lime backgrounds: all-ink mono mark — lime-on-lime disappears.
  • Blue or photographic backgrounds: all-white mono mark. On photography, place only over calm, low-detail areas.
  • One-color reproduction (engraving, embossing, fax, stamp): mono-black or mono-white; keystone takes the ring color.
  • Lime never carries the ring, and the keystone is never blue. In any single use of the mark exactly two colors appear at most.

4 · Typography

  • Wordmark: Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold (600), letter-spacing −0.05 em, with two kerning corrections baked into the artwork: g–n tightened −0.022 em, i–s tightened −0.012 em. Always title case: "Cognis". Never retype the wordmark in running text styling — use the supplied artwork.
  • GROUP suffix: Geist Mono Medium, all caps, letter-spacing +0.52 em, set to the wordmark's left edge, ~27% of the wordmark's point size. It is a fixed part of the primary lockup — never restyle or recolor it independently.
  • Brand text: Plus Jakarta Sans for headings and body; Geist Mono for labels, eyebrow text, and technical annotation. In email, fall back to Arial / Helvetica.

5 · Clearspace & minimum sizes

Cognis
  • Clearspace: half the ring diameter on all four sides, measured from the lockup's bounding box. Nothing enters this zone — text, other logos, page edges, busy imagery.
  • Minimum sizes: mark alone 15 px / 5 mm; short lockup 20 px / 7 mm tall; primary lockup 34 px / 12 mm tall (below that GROUP becomes illegible — switch to the short lockup).
  • At 16 px and below use the supplied favicon files, which carry an enlarged keystone for legibility.

6 · Motion

The mark has one motion behavior — a reveal: the ring sweeps in with a rotation (~110° with an ease-out), then the keystone snaps into the notch with a slight overshoot. Total duration ~3 seconds. Motion is a moment, not a state: in real use the animation plays once, then the mark holds static. The sample at left loops here only for study; in print it appears as the static mark.

7 · Misuse — never do these

✕Don't rotate — the notch faces right ✕Don't close the ring or drop the keystone ✕Don't recolor the keystone — lime, or the ring color in mono ✕Don't stretch, squash, or skew ✕Don't place on gradients or busy imagery ✕Don't add shadows, outlines, glows, or effects Cognis✕Don't retype the wordmark in another font or weight Cognis✕Don't recolor the wordmark — ink or white only
