Process

How we deliver

Many AI plans begin with technology and only later ask which business problem it should solve. That creates expensive trials that never become part of everyday work.

We begin with your goals, processes, people, data and risks. We then identify the opportunities worth funding and turn them into a practical plan with clear owners, costs, controls and measures of success.

1

Check what is ready.

We review your data, systems, people and existing rules against the work you want to improve.

2

Choose the right opportunities.

We rank ideas by business value, cost, difficulty, speed and risk.

3

Agree how the work will run.

We define who funds, owns, approves and measures each AI project.

4

Build the roadmap.

You receive a phased plan with named owners, dependencies and decision points.

5

Prepare leaders to decide.

We provide a clear investment case, expected results and a plain view of the risks.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does an AI strategy engagement take?

Most engagements take 8–14 weeks. We first assess what is ready, then agree priorities, ownership and controls before presenting the final plan to leadership.

Do you build the AI systems you recommend?

Yes, if you want us to. You can use Cognis for the strategy only, the build only, or the full journey. Our advice is based on what the organisation needs.

What makes the Cognis approach practical?

Every recommendation names the business problem, owner, data, cost, risk controls and measure of success. The result is a plan your team can act on.

Which laws and standards do you consider?

Where relevant, we work with Nigeria's Data Protection Act, the EU AI Act, ISO 42001, NIST's AI Risk Management Framework and sector-specific rules. We explain what each one means for your plan.

Can you advise whether to buy or build an AI platform?

Yes. We compare buying, building and combining both options against your needs, data-location rules, budget and in-house skills.

Do you work with organisations outside Africa?

Yes. We are based in Lagos and work across Africa and beyond, especially where organisations need strong local understanding alongside global AI expertise.
