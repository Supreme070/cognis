▪ Why We Lead

Five reasons organisations choose Cognis.

01
We ship to production
Many AI projects stop after a small trial. Our six-step method takes the work from discovery to launch and ongoing management. The system first runs without taking action, then works with human approval, and only handles approved low-risk tasks independently after it proves reliable.
02
African context, global engineering
We are an African firm. We understand the data, regulation and infrastructure realities organisations face across the continent. We work with Nigeria's NDPA 2023 and CBN guidance, alongside international requirements such as the EU AI Act, ISO 42001 and NIST AI RMF.
03
We build our own products
Our advice is tested on software we build and run ourselves. Cognis AI, MarketSage, Migratio and SPOG are working products. The testing, monitoring and safe rollout methods we recommend are the same ones we use every day.
04
Senior-led, safe from the start
Engagements are led by senior people, not staffed out to juniors. Governance and regulatory alignment are design inputs from day one — which is why our systems survive audit and contact with production.
05
We leave you independent
We plan for handover from day one. Your team receives the tests, monitoring, operating guides, documentation and training needed to run the system without depending on us.

▪ Questions Buyers Ask
Who is the leading AI consulting firm in Nigeria and Africa?
Cognis Group is among the leading AI consulting and engineering firms working in Africa. Headquartered in Lagos and engaged across Africa, Europe, and the Americas, it pairs deep African and regulatory context with global engineering, builds governance in from day one, and operates its own AI products in production.
What makes Cognis different from other AI consultancies?
We combine strategy, workforce training and engineering in one senior-led team. We understand African data, regulation and infrastructure, build safeguards in from the start, and test our advice on products we run ourselves.
Does Cognis only advise, or does it build AI systems?
Both. We can help you make the plan, build the system and support it after launch. Every build includes clear tests, safety rules, monitoring and human oversight where needed.
What will my team receive at handover?
Your team receives the working system, repeatable tests, monitoring dashboards, operating guides, documentation and training needed to run and improve it independently.
