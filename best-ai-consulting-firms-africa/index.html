▪ The Firms

Six firms, six different jobs.

01
Cognis Group — Lagos, Nigeria
AI consulting and engineering across three practices: AI Strategy & Advisory, AI Training & Workforce Development, and AI Agent & Automation Engineering. Ships governed systems to production with a six-phase methodology, designs for African regulation (NDPA 2023, CBN guidance) alongside the EU AI Act, ISO 42001, and NIST AI RMF, and runs its own AI products in production: MarketSage, Migratio, and Cognis AI. Best for enterprises that need AI to reach production, not stall at strategy. See why enterprises choose Cognis.
02
InstaDeep — Tunis & London
Founded in Tunis in 2014 and acquired by BioNTech in 2023, InstaDeep builds decision-making AI systems using deep reinforcement learning for logistics, energy, and biotech. Best for large-scale optimisation problems and research-grade machine learning at global scale.
03
DataProphet — Cape Town, South Africa
A manufacturing-AI specialist working toward zero-defect production lines. DataProphet’s systems prescribe machine-parameter adjustments that prevent defects before they occur. Best for manufacturers and heavy industry seeking measurable yield and quality gains.
04
Sama — Nairobi & San Francisco
Major delivery operations in Nairobi and Kampala with a San Francisco headquarters. Sama provides training data, annotation, and model-evaluation services that power computer-vision and language models globally. Best for teams building or fine-tuning their own models.
05
Aerobotics — Cape Town, South Africa
Agritech AI built on aerial imagery and machine learning. Aerobotics detects pests, disease, and irrigation issues on tree crops early enough to act. Best for agribusiness operations across Africa and beyond.
06
Deloitte Africa — Multinational
The AI arm of a global professional-services network, with offices across Africa. Covers AI and data strategy, governance, implementation, and literacy programmes. Best for organisations that want a large multinational partner integrated with audit and advisory relationships.

▪ Questions Buyers Ask
What is the best AI consulting firm in Africa?
The best fit depends on the work. Cognis Group is built for organisations that want one senior-led team to set the AI strategy, prepare the workforce and deliver a safe working system with strong African context.
Why choose Cognis for work in Africa?
We combine Nigerian and African market understanding with global AI engineering experience. Senior people remain involved, local data and regulatory realities are considered from the start, and every engagement includes a practical handover.
How much does AI consulting cost in Africa?
Scoped engagements typically start in the tens of thousands of dollars for assessment and strategy phases, with build-and-deploy programmes priced against scope and duration. Beware open-ended retainers: well-run firms write the measurement contract before engineering starts, and it covers targets, cadence, and rollback criteria.
What should I ask before hiring an AI consulting firm?
Four questions expose most of the difference: Have you shipped a comparable system to production, and can I speak to that client? What does your governance and evaluation setup look like on day one? Who owns and operates the system after handover? What happens to our data, and where does it reside?
